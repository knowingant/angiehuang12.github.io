Minnesota Youth Math Outreach

This summer, we completed our third season of free math programs, including an online intensive competition preparation camp, an in-person competition, and an in-person girls' event!

Camp Description

Learn fundamental skills as well as advanced topics to prepare for math competitions like the AMCs and AIME! Our free online camp is targeted for middle-school students and older, but strong younger students are invited to attend as well. Our classes are taught by top mathletes from around the state, including the captains of the Minnesota All-State Math Team. Our beginner group covers each of the four main subject areas (algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory) in three days, with three days devoted to miscellaneous topics. Our advanced group focuses for seven to eight days on each of two subject areas.
