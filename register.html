    • MNYMO Camp

    Our online camp took place from August 12th to 27th. Students chose between two class groups based on their familiarity with concepts. Students learned concepts from the four main subject areas of competition math (Algebra, Combinatorics, Number Theory, and Geometry), each of which was covered with one lesson on fundamentals and two lessons on advanced topics (inequalities and complex numbers for Algebra, perspectives and recursion for Combinatorics, theorems and tricks for Number Theory, and mass points and trigonometry for Geometry). We had so much fun with group problem solving sessions and activities!

    Stay tuned for details about next year's camp!

    • Why Us?

    • Summer is a great time to get back into competition math preparation! We believe that our teaching and resources will not only help prepare students, but also inspire them to take on their own initiatives.
